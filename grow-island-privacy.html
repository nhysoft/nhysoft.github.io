Grow Island – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the game Grow Island (shown as "Island" on your home screen; Android package com.madmaxoo.light, iOS bundle com.nhysoft.growisland) handles your information. Grow Island is developed by DUNV (nhysoft).

Information we collect

None. Grow Island does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The app has no account or login, and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the app saves your game progress and settings (selected language, sound on/off, whether you have seen the tutorial) only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Internet, advertising and analytics

Grow Island works fully offline. It does not use the internet, and contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases and no third-party services.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions.

Children

Grow Island is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
